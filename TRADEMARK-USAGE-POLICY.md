**Status:** Normative

**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

The Pale Blue Systems Foundation (PBSF) holds the trademarks for the Pale Blue Systems Open Standard to ensure that the community, industry, and public can trust the source and quality of the technical specifications and associated tools.

This policy defines how the names, logos, and related marks of the Foundation and the Standard may be used by third parties.

## 2. The Trademarks

The following terms are trademarks of the Pale Blue Systems Foundation:

* **Pale Blue Systems™**
* **PBSF™**
* **PBS Open Standard™**
* **Pale Blue Systems Core™**
* The **Pale Blue Systems Logo** (and any visual variants published by the Foundation)

## 3. Permitted Use (Fair Use)

You **may** use the trademarks without prior written permission in the following contexts, provided the use is truthful and not misleading:

* **Referential Use:** referring to the standard itself (e.g., "This device implements the PBS Open Standard").
* **Documentation:** citing the standard in academic papers, technical documentation, or architectural diagrams.
* **Compatibility Statements:** stating that a product is "interoperable with" or "compatible with" the PBS Open Standard, provided the product fully conforms to `PBS-CONFORMANCE-01`.

## 4. Prohibited Use (Infringement)

You **may not** use the trademarks in the following ways without a specific license or certification agreement:

* **Product Naming:** You may not name your product, service, or company in a way that suggests it is an official product of the Foundation or Pale Blue Systems Inc. (e.g., naming a product "The PBS Router" is prohibited; naming it "Router X, for PBS" is permitted).
* **Endorsement:** You may not use the marks in a way that implies endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with the Foundation where none exists.
* **Confusing Similarity:** You may not use a variation of the marks or logos that is confusingly similar to the official marks.
* **False Conformance:** You may not claim compatibility if your product does not adhere to the mandatory requirements of `PBS-CONFORMANCE-01`.

## 5. Certification and "Official" Status

The Foundation may, at its discretion, offer a **Certification Program**. Only products that have passed official validation and signed a Certification Agreement may display the "PBS Certified" logo or use specific designation marks reserved for certified hardware/software.

## 6. Updates

The Foundation reserves the right to modify this policy at any time.

For questions regarding trademark usage, please contact the Foundation governance body.
