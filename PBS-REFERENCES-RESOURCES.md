## References, Standards, and Supporting Resources

**Status:** Informational  
**Applies to:** Pale Blue Systems Open Standard (PBS)

---

## 1. Purpose

This document provides **authoritative references and external resources** that inform, validate, and support the design and mission of the Pale Blue Systems Open Standard (PBS).

The resources below fall into three categories:
1. **Foundational protocols and technologies** PBS aligns with or interoperates with  
2. **Publicly documented needs and shortfalls** identified by space agencies  
3. **Programs, missions, and initiatives**—public and commercial—that demonstrate the relevance and necessity of PBS-style solutions  

All links reference publicly available, validated sources.

---

## 2. Foundational Networking Protocols and Standards

### Delay/Disruption Tolerant Networking (DTN)

- **RFC 4838 – Delay-Tolerant Networking Architecture**  
  https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc4838  

- **RFC 9171 – Bundle Protocol Version 7 (BPv7)**  
  https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc9171  

- **RFC 5050 (Historical) – Bundle Protocol Specification**  
  https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc5050  

DTN provides the architectural basis for store-and-forward communication in environments with long delays and intermittent connectivity. PBS is designed to align with DTN at system boundaries without imposing DTN semantics internally.

---

### CCSDS (Consultative Committee for Space Data Systems)

- **CCSDS Blue Books (Official Standards Repository)**  
  https://public.ccsds.org/Publications/BlueBooks.aspx  

- **CCSDS Space Internetworking Overview**

CCSDS standards form the backbone of interoperable space communications across international agencies.

## 3. NASA and JPL DTN Implementations

### Interplanetary Overlay Network (ION)

ION is NASA/JPL’s reference implementation of DTN for space missions.

- **ION DTN Overview (NASA/JPL)**  
  https://ipnsig.jpl.nasa.gov/  

- **ION GitHub Repository (NASA/JPL)**  
  https://github.com/nasa-jpl/ION-DTN  

ION demonstrates operational DTN across flight and ground systems and serves as a primary interoperability target for PBS-DTN-MAP.

---

### NASA DTN and Networking Research

- **NASA Space Communications and Navigation (SCaN) Program**  
  https://www.nasa.gov/space-communications-navigation  

- **NASA DTN Research and Demonstrations**  
  https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/heo/scan/engineering/DTN  

These efforts validate DTN as a critical component of future space architectures.
